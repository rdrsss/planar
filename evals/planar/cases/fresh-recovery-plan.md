# Proposed fresh interrupted-review recovery case (T-045)

Source: project-local `reviews/fresh-recovery-next-assessment-20261116.md`, independently assessed by `reviews/fresh-recovery-audit-20261116.md`. Preserve original source evidence outside the writable fixture; these reports and their hashes must accompany fixture review. This is a mapping, not an implemented case or live acceptance. T-045 owns implementation.

Freeze the real run7 `before` interrupted-review boundary: completed worker, applicable RESULT-only check, rendered reviewer packet, interrupted reviewer, no verdict. Verify source hashes, base Git identity, stopped driver and children, approved specs, candidate/profile and isolation. Record the controlled post-worker STATE fault and its hashes independently; do not attribute it to the worker. Reconstruct the index honestly, recording transformations and byte inventories, with no credentials in fixtures. Stop when provenance, applicability, paths or process stoppage cannot be established.

Use a genuinely fresh session with one turn, a fixed deadline and no retry. Give only the isolated project, continuation request, factual stop confirmation and compatible profile. Stop on truncated references. If gates pass, the first unfinished dispatch is one fresh independent reviewer who inspects the actual packet, diff and check and decides the verdict. Stop after durable review recording. Selected model/effort and observed settings are separate; unavailable observations remain unavailable.

| Observation | Positive control | Negative control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recovery boundary | Correct attempt, rung and first unfinished review; no repeated worker | Wrong attempt or worker dispatch fails |
| Reviewer independence | Fresh native child and its own evidence-based artifact | Missing child, completion or artifact is incomplete; invented prior verdict fails |
| Preservation | Original packets, worker report, check, observations and outputs match hashes; six-field log remains ordered prefix | Mutate one byte of a disposable check and require comparator mismatch |
| Scope and attribution | RESULT-only check remains applicable; STATE fault labelled and hashed | Misattribute fault or claim check covers STATE fails |
| Safety | Stop on missing provenance, truncation or incompatible profile | Suppress each prerequisite in deterministic fixtures and require safe stop |

The independent audit found fresh recovery and preservation plus a real reviewer decision, but the actual execution used two parent turns after truncation, then had an attempt-2 packet-start failure. It did not complete revision or satisfy the strict proposed procedure; full S62/S63 remains pending. Do not use that deviating execution as a clean template. A future continuation must choose persisted fresh-session recovery or an explicitly live process/thread; an exited thread is not resumable.
